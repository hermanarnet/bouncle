Bouncle support

Three trick-shot challenges a day, the same for everyone, plus 100 Adventure levels. Fewest shots wins.

How do I play?

Drag back anywhere on the screen like a slingshot, then let go. Bounce the ball off rails, bumpers, spinners and portals until it lands in the cup. You have 9 shots.

When does a new level arrive?

Every day at midnight in your own time zone. Everyone in the world plays the same level on the same day.

How do streaks work?

Your streak grows every day you hole the level. Miss a day, or run out of shots, and it starts again. Longer streaks unlock new balls.

What are ghosts?

When a friend shares their result, the link carries their winning shot. Open it and, after your own first shot, you can watch their ghost replay the shot on your screen.
