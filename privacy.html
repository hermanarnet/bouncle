Privacy Policy

Bouncle is made by Herman Arnet. Last updated: October 2026.

The short version

Bouncle does not collect, store or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your progress (shots, streaks, statistics, unlocked balls, settings and the display name you choose) is saved only on your own device. Deleting the app or clearing website data removes it.

Share links

When you tap Share, the game creates a link that contains the day number, your score, your winning shot and the display name you chose. That link is only sent where you decide to send it. The shot data sits in the part of the link after the #, which browsers never send to any server.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, your daily score is submitted to a Game Center leaderboard. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer only sees what Apple shows on public leaderboards.

Children

Bouncle is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
